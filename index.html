DSWD Program References

Official policy, case-management guidance, and knowledge resources to guide program-specific encoding.

Reference library
Important: These references guide the design of the system's fields and workflow; they are not a substitute for the latest official DSWD Field Office V reporting templates, prescribed forms, or instructions. Confirm required fields and quarterly indicators with the responsible DSWD office before using reports officially.

CNSP — Children in Need of Special Protection

Child protection, abuse/exploitation, reporting, referral, legal proceedings, and intervention tracking.

Suggested encoding areas: referral source, child-protection concern, immediate safety/protection action, case assessment, legal/prosecution status, placement or child-caring institution referral, interventions, and follow-up.

DSWD: Revised protocol for child victims of abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination (CANE+D)

DSWD/DROMIC: Flowchart of reporting for children in need of special protection

VAWC — Violence Against Women and their Children

Gender-responsive, survivor-centered case management and coordinated services.

Suggested encoding areas: type of violence, relationship to alleged perpetrator, safety and protection needs, risk/needs assessment, referral and service plan, protection-order/legal status, interdisciplinary referrals, and follow-up. Limit sensitive narrative and identifying details to authorized records.

DSWD Social Technology Bureau: Gender-Responsive Case Management (GRCM)

DSWD Knowledge Management Portal: GRCM Trainers' Manual for VAW Survivors

DSWD Standards Bureau: VAWC reporting, protection orders, and referral pathways

CICL — Children in Conflict with the Law

Child-sensitive justice, assessment, diversion, intervention, rehabilitation, and reintegration.

Suggested encoding areas: incident/referral details, age at time of alleged offense, assessment and discernment-related process where applicable, diversion level/status, court/prosecutor status, intervention plan, family participation, progress reports, and reintegration/aftercare. Avoid treating a child as convicted based solely on an allegation.

DSWD Administrative Order No. 10, s. 2007: Guidelines for Social Workers in Handling and Treatment of CICL

DSWD Knowledge Management Portal: Buklod Paglaom / HoPE Interventions for CICL Program Manual

DSWD: Proper handling and management of CICL cases

CAR — Children at Risk

Early identification of risk, assessment, family/community support, referral, and prevention.

Suggested encoding areas: risk indicators, source of identification, child and family assessment, immediate safety concerns, strengths/protective factors, case plan, referrals, family/community interventions, and review dates. CAR must remain distinguishable from CICL; classification should follow applicable law and DSWD/JJWC guidance.

DSWD: Compendium of DSWD and Selected Social Welfare and Development Statistics (definitions including Child at Risk)

Revised IRR of RA 9344, as amended by RA 10630 (community-based programs and rehabilitation)

Before approving official reports

  1. Obtain the current program-specific forms and quarterly reporting indicators from DSWD Field Office V or the designated provincial/regional focal person.
  2. Map each official field to the corresponding encoding field; retain official labels and required breakdowns.
  3. Keep aggregate quarterly counts separate from individual case records to avoid double counting carry-over cases.
  4. Apply least-privilege access, audit logs, secure file storage, and approved retention/disposal rules before encoding real client information.
  5. Record the reference title, issuing office, issuance number/date, version, and date verified whenever an official form or policy is updated.
